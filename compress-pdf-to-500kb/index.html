Why reduce a PDF to 500 KB?

A 500 KB target is useful for many websites, online forms and application portals that restrict document uploads.

PDF compression and quality

Smaller targets can require stronger compression. The tool attempts to preserve quality first, but a very large reduction may affect image clarity or text selection.

Frequently asked questions

How do I compress a PDF to 500 KB?

Open the compressor, choose your PDF and use the 500 KB target. Click Compress and download the result when processing finishes.

Can every PDF be reduced to exactly 500 KB?

Not every PDF can reach an exact target without significant quality or content changes. The compressor attempts to get close to the target while preserving quality first.

Will my PDF text remain selectable?

If a lossless re-save can reach the target, text can remain selectable. When stronger compression is needed, pages may be converted to images and text selection may no longer work.
