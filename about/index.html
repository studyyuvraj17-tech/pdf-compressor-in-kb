About PDFCompressInKB

PDFCompressInKB is a simple online PDF compression tool built for people who need to reduce a PDF to a specific file-size limit such as 100 KB, 200 KB, 500 KB or 1 MB.

Our goal

The goal is to make target-size PDF compression easy to understand and use without requiring desktop software.

How the tool works

The compressor runs in the user's web browser and uses browser-based PDF processing to try to reduce the document toward the selected target size.

Quality matters

The tool attempts a lossless optimization first. If stronger compression is required, pages may be rendered as images, which can affect text selection and visual quality.

Important limitation

PDFs vary greatly. A particular document may not be able to reach a requested target without substantial quality changes.
