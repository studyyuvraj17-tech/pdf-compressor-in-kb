Contact PDFCompressInKB

Have a question, found a problem, or want to report an issue with PDFCompressInKB?

Support

Use the contact method provided by the website owner when available. We do not publish a personal address or phone number on this page.

What to include

  • The page or feature you were using.
  • Your browser and device.
  • A short description of the problem.
  • If relevant, the approximate PDF size and number of pages. Do not send confidential documents unless you are comfortable doing so.

Privacy requests

If you need to ask about privacy or data handling, clearly label your request as a privacy request.
