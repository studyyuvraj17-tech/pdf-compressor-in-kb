Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PDFCompressInKB handles information when you use the website.

PDF files

The PDF compressor is designed to process files in your browser. The normal compression workflow does not intentionally upload the PDF you select to a PDF-compression server.

Your browser may temporarily hold the file and generated data in memory while you use the tool. Downloaded files remain under your control.

Website usage data

If analytics or advertising services are enabled, technical information such as browser type, device information, approximate location, pages visited and referral information may be processed by those services.

Analytics

The website currently uses Google Analytics to understand aggregate website usage, such as pages visited and general interaction with the service. Google may use cookies or similar technologies to provide its analytics service. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy.

Advertising and Google AdSense

If Google AdSense or another advertising service is enabled on this website, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or similar technologies to serve, measure and limit advertising, detect fraud, and understand ad performance. Depending on the settings, applicable law and your consent choices, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use information from visits to this and other websites to provide advertising services.

You can learn about Google's advertising practices and manage available ad-personalization settings at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and My Ad Center. The availability of settings depends on your region and account.

Where required by law or Google's publisher policies, the website will provide an appropriate consent mechanism before relevant advertising technologies are used. Advertising services are not represented as active unless they have actually been enabled.

Third-party services and external links

The website loads third-party libraries required for PDF processing and may load analytics, advertising or other services. Those providers may receive technical information from your browser and process it under their own privacy policies. The PDF compression workflow is designed to process your selected PDF locally in your browser; this does not mean that all website requests or analytics are local.

Cookies and choices

Cookies and similar technologies may be used by analytics, advertising or other services. You can manage cookies through your browser settings, although disabling them may affect some website features. Where legally required, a suitable consent mechanism will be used for applicable cookies and advertising technologies.

Data retention and contact

PDFCompressInKB does not intentionally upload the selected PDF to a PDF-compression server during the normal compression workflow. Analytics and third-party requests are separate from the local PDF processing workflow. For questions about this policy, use the Contact page.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the website, analytics, advertising or processing architecture changes.
