How PDFCompressInKB Works

1. Choose a PDF

Select a PDF from your device or drag it into the compressor.

2. Set your target

Choose a target such as 100 KB, 200 KB, 500 KB or 1 MB.

3. Optimize the PDF

The tool first tries a lossless PDF re-save. If that is not enough, it uses stronger image-based compression with different rendering scales and JPEG quality levels.

4. Build the compressed PDF

When image-based compression is required, pages are rendered in the browser and used to create a new PDF.

5. Download and check

When processing finishes, download the result and verify that the pages look correct and that the document meets the upload requirements of your destination.

Privacy by design

The normal compression workflow is designed to process your PDF locally in the browser rather than sending the PDF to a remote compression server.
