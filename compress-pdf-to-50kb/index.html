Frequently Asked Questions

How can I compress a PDF to 50 KB?

Upload your PDF and use 50 KB as the target. The tool checks the result, but some PDFs cannot be reduced to the requested size without unacceptable quality loss.

Will every PDF compress to 50 KB?

Not always. The result depends on the PDF's contents. Check the final size and readability before submitting it.

Will my PDF text remain selectable?

A lossless re-save preserves selectable text when it reaches the target. Stronger compression may convert pages into images, which can limit text selection.

Is this PDF compressor free?

Yes. You can compress and download PDFs for free in a modern browser with JavaScript enabled.
